03 MISSION CONTROL

Missions & practice briefs

Real work, self-set exercises and course-led experiments. Each mission records its objective, approach and outcome — including what it did not achieve.

9 records Evidence-led No invented results

How missions are recorded

Recording rules for this module

  • Practice work is labelled. Self-set briefs are marked SPEC BRIEF or LEARNING EXERCISE — never as client work.
  • No metrics are invented. Where results were not measured, the metrics panel stays empty and says so.
  • Evidence is optional but explicit. If a link or document exists it is listed; if not, the empty state explains what is missing.
  • Archived work stays visible. Superseded work is archived rather than deleted, because the gap is the useful part.
