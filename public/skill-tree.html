// METHOD

How levels are assessed

Self-assessed, and labelled as such

These levels are my own assessment against the definitions above. There is no external audit, no test score and no certificate behind them — and the interface says so rather than implying otherwise.

What would change a level

A level moves when the work changes: completing a real brief at that standard, using the skill unaided under a deadline, or being able to explain the decision to someone else. Time spent on a course does not move a level on its own.
