What qualifies for this register

Listed

  • Completed courses with a verifiable certificate or credential URL
  • Formal programmes with an issuer who can confirm completion
  • Credentials marked REFERENCED or PENDING with the reason stated

Not listed

  • Courses started but not finished
  • Self-study, tutorials and reading (those live in the Learning Lab)
  • Anything without an issuer or a way to verify completion

How to add one

Add an object to the achievements array in content/achievements.js with type: 'CERTIFICATE', the issuer, the date, a credential object and verification.status. It will appear here and in achievements automatically — no component changes required.
