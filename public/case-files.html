// EVIDENCE POLICY

What “case file” means here

A case file is a structured record of work, not a promise of a commercial result.

WHAT IS RECORDED

  • Objective, context and the approach actually taken
  • Outcome, including limits and things that did not work
  • Skills and tools used, linked to the skill tree and toolkit
  • Lessons that changed how the next project was handled

WHAT IS NOT

  • Invented client names, budgets or revenue figures
  • Conversion rates, rankings or traffic that was never measured
  • Testimonials from people who have not given them
  • Awards, certifications or partnerships that do not exist
